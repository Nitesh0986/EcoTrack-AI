# EcoTrack-AI
An AI-powered carbon footprint tracking platform that helps users monitor emissions, receive personalized sustainability recommendations, and build eco-friendly habits through actionable insights and progress analytics.
# EcoTrack-AI 🌱

## Overview

EcoTrack-AI is an intelligent sustainability assistant designed to help individuals understand, track, and reduce their carbon footprint through personalized insights and actionable recommendations.

The platform analyzes daily activities such as transportation, electricity consumption, food habits, and waste generation to estimate carbon emissions and suggest practical ways to adopt a more sustainable lifestyle.

---

## Problem Statement

Design a solution that helps individuals understand, track, and reduce their carbon footprint through simple actions and personalized insights.

---

## Features

### Carbon Footprint Calculator

* Calculate daily and monthly carbon emissions
* Track emissions from transportation, energy usage, food habits, and waste generation
* Generate an overall carbon score

### AI Sustainability Assistant

* Personalized recommendations based on user behavior
* AI-powered suggestions to reduce emissions
* Context-aware sustainability guidance

### Progress Dashboard

* Visual representation of carbon footprint trends
* Weekly and monthly analytics
* Performance tracking and improvement monitoring

### Eco Challenges

* Daily and weekly sustainability tasks
* Reward-based engagement system
* Habit-building activities

### Carbon Score System

* High Emission
* Moderate Emission
* Good
* Eco Hero

### Smart Reports

* Monthly sustainability summaries
* Personalized improvement suggestions
* Progress insights

## How It Works

1. User enters daily activity information.
2. The system calculates estimated carbon emissions.
3. AI analyzes the data and generates personalized suggestions.
4. Dashboard visualizes progress and trends.
5. Users complete eco-friendly challenges to improve their sustainability score.
